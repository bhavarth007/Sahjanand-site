Integrated Manufacturing Facilities

Strategically situated in Gujarat's premier industrial corridors across Sachin GIDC and Hojiwala, our production infrastructure ensures seamless sourcing, high production throughput, and rapid dispatch across India.

Plant Unit 01 • Weaving Segment

High-Speed Weaving Facility

Road No. 43-B, Sachin GIDC, Surat

Our primary weaving powerhouse engineered for high-density, multi-shuttle, and shuttleless synthetic fabric production. Equipped with high-speed Rapier Looms, Electronic Jacquard machines, and advanced Waterjet & Airjet looms with electronic dobby shedding for multi-color weft selections across reed widths from 190 cm to 340 cm.

  • Key Machinery: High-Speed Rapier, Jacquard, Waterjet & Airjet Looms
  • Fabric Output: Synthetic Dress Materials, Dobby Weaves, Micro-Poly & Crepe
  • Quality Standards: Optical Warp-Stop Sensors & 4-Point Roll Grading
Plant Unit 02 • Yarn Segment

Yarn Texturising, Twisting & Processing Plant

Hojiwala Industrial Estate, Surat

Dedicated continuous draw-texturising (DTY) and high-precision Two-For-One (TFO) twisting plant. Operates multi-spindle texturising machines with ceramic friction discs and intermingling air jets for producing zero-defect roto, crimp, and precision-twisted filament yarns across counts from 30D to 300D.

  • Key Machinery: High-Speed DTY Texturising & Multi-Spindle TFO Twisters
  • Yarn Capabilities: DTY (Semi-Dull, Bright), Crimp, Roto & Kota Yarns
  • Specialized Counts: 17×24 & 42×24 Nylon TPM, 40 FD TPM, Slit-3 Muskaan
Plant Unit 03 • Chemical Segment

Specialty Chemical & Sizing Auxiliary Unit

Sachin Industrial Corridor, Surat

Formulation and synthesis facility for high-performance sizing binders, modified starch blends, and wet-processing textile auxiliaries. Features temperature-controlled stainless-steel blending reactors, high-shear homogenizers, and closed-loop liquid dispensing tanks ensuring uniform viscosity and active solids concentration.

  • Key Machinery: SS Reaction Vessels & High-Shear Blending Reactors
  • Formulations: Eco Sizing Starches, PVA Binders, Defoamers & Auxiliaries
  • Environmental Standards: Zero-Discharge Protocol & Eco-Safe Formulations
Plant Unit 04 • Trade Segment

Central Warehousing & Global Trade Logistics Hub

Sachin GIDC Logistics Zone, Surat

Modern high-capacity covered inventory warehousing and global trade indenting terminal. Houses heavy-tonnage buffer stocks of virgin polyester chips, nylon filaments, specialty industrial polymers, and finished fabric consignments with automated palletizing and multi-bay truck loading docks.

  • Key Equipment: Electric Forklifts, Multi-Tier Stacking Racks & Barcode ERP
  • Staging Capacity: Over 150,000 sq. ft Organized Covered Storage
  • Connectivity: Direct Link to NH-48 & Hazira Port Corridor for India-Wide Freight
In-House Laboratories

Quality Control & Testing Infrastructure

Nothing departs our plants without verified laboratory test certificates. Our quality assurance centers operate calibrated digital testing equipment monitored by certified textile technologists and process engineers.

Yarn Physical & Tensile Lab

Features digital Lea strength testers, electronic twist testers for precise TPM verification, wrap reels for denier count calibration, and Uster evenness and elongation meters ensuring consistent yarn package performance.

Fabric Physical & Weave Lab

Equipped with digital GSM balance cutters, Elmendorf tear strength testers, fabric tensile grab testers, digital pick-glass thread counters (EPI & PPI verification), and dimensional stability/shrinkage baths.

Chemical Synthesis & Color Lab

Features Brookfield rotational viscometers, digital pH transmitters, refractometers for solids/Brix concentration, sizing pickup titration units, foam collapse kinetic benches, and crockmeters for colorfastness.

Handling & Dispatch

Temperature-Controlled Storage & Fleet Logistics

To ensure flawless product preservation, Sahjanand maintains dedicated humidity-conditioned storage for raw and texturised yarns, clean roll-wrapping bays for grey and finished fabrics, and high-clearance covered docks for seamless multi-axle freight loading.

Our logistical dispatch coordination ensures that packed fabric rolls, yarn cartons, and chemical containers are dispatched with strict invoice-matching and delivery challan verification directly to clients across Surat and major industrial centers across India.

  • Staging Capacity: Over 150,000 sq. ft of organized yards and warehouses
  • Handling Equipment: Electric forklifts, hydraulic pallet trucks & automated wrap lines
  • Dispatch Turnaround: Prompt dispatch coordination with dedicated gate entry & challan tracking
