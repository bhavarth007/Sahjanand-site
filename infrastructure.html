Integrated Manufacturing Facilities

Strategically situated in Gujarat's major industrial corridors, our production infrastructure ensures streamlined sourcing, high production throughput, and rapid dispatch across India.

Plant Unit 01

Surat Concrete Products Facility

Surat District, Gujarat

Our flagship concrete works operates a fully automated computerized batching plant (60 m³/hr capacity) coupled with high-pressure vibro-compaction presses. Dedicated steam-curing chambers guarantee accelerated, uniform hydration and high early compressive strength.

  • Key Machinery: Automated Block Machine & Vibro-Press
  • Monthly Capacity: 12,000 Square Meters Pavers
  • Standards: Tested to IS 15658:2006 Standards
Plant Unit 02

Hazira Structural Steel Fabrication Bay

Hazira Industrial Belt, Gujarat

Spanning multi-bay covered fabrication halls with twin 25-tonne EOT gantry cranes, heavy plate bending rolls, CNC multi-head oxy-fuel/plasma cutting tables, and automatic submerged arc welding lines for large-span structural elements.

  • Key Machinery: CNC Plasma Cutters & Submerged Arc Welders
  • Annual Capacity: 8,500 Metric Tonnes Fabricated Steel
  • Standards: IS 2062 Grade E250 / E350 Structural Steel
Plant Unit 03

Ankleshwar Precast Concrete Works

Ankleshwar GIDC, Gujarat

Engineered for precision municipal and industrial civil precast elements, including box culverts, heavy manhole rings, and drainage channels. Includes self-compacting concrete mixers, steel formworks, and hydraulic demoulding stations.

  • Key Machinery: Precast Tilting Tables & Heavy Shuttering
  • Annual Output: 14,000 Cubic Meters Precast Units
  • Standards: IS 458 & IRC Special Load Guidelines
Plant Unit 04

Sachin Industrial Fittings & Utility Plant

Sachin Industrial Zone, Gujarat

Specialized molding, threading, and fusion joining plant for high-density polyethylene (HDPE) fittings and heavy galvanized iron utility network components. Features a closed-loop pressure testing bay for hydrostatic quality assurance.

  • Key Machinery: HDPE Butt Fusion & Injection Molding Rigs
  • Pressure Rating: Pressure Rated to 25 Bar Hydrostatic
  • Standards: IS 4984 / ISO 4427 Certified Quality
In-House Laboratories

Quality Control & Testing Infrastructure

Nothing departs our plants without verified laboratory test certificates. Our quality assurance centers operate calibrated digital testing equipment monitored by certified metallurgists and civil engineers.

Compression Testing Lab

Features 2,000 kN digital compressive load machines calibrated to test concrete block fracture thresholds, paver flexural resilience, and precast core strengths against IS standard curves.

Metallurgical & Tensile Lab

Equipped with computerized Universal Testing Machines (UTM) to verify yield strength, ultimate tensile strength, elongation, and ultrasonic flaw detection on structural steel welds.

Hydrostatic Pressure Bench

A specialized multi-station hydro-test bench testing HDPE and GI pipe fittings up to 25 bar sustained pressure to ensure zero porosity, leak resistance, and burst endurance.

Handling & Dispatch

Heavy Yard Storage & Fleet Logistics

To support high-volume industrial and commercial requirements, Sahjanand maintains extensive covered and open stacking yards with dedicated gantry overhead handling.

Our logistical dispatch coordination ensures that precast components, heavy steel members, and bulk palletized pavers are transported safely and directly to industrial sites across Gujarat and neighboring states.

  • Staging Capacity: Over 150,000 sq. ft of organized yards
  • Handling Equipment: Mobile boom cranes, heavy forklifts & EOT cranes
  • Dispatch Turnaround: Same-day dispatch for standard stocked materials
