A Purpose-Led Enterprise Driven by Engineering Discipline and Trust.

Founded on the principle behind our name — ease, and the absolute trust that comes from it — Sahjanand has evolved into a premier industrial manufacturing enterprise. Operating across four integrated production facilities in Gujarat, we produce engineered construction products built to endure.

We work close to our manufacturing floor: our engineers, metallurgists, and plant supervisors are directly involved in every production batch to ensure complete consistency from raw material testing to final dispatch.

Four Specialized Manufacturing Units

Each unit operates as an integrated production center equipped with dedicated testing laboratories, automated batching plants, and precision forming machinery.

Manufacturing Unit 01

Concrete Products & Heavy-Duty Pavers

Surat District, Gujarat

Equipped with computerized batching plant, high-frequency vibro-compaction presses, and steam curing chambers for heavy-duty industrial interlocking pavers and masonry blocks.

  • Production Output: 12,000 sq. meters/month
  • Compression Rating: M30 to M50 Grade
  • Standard Compliance: IS 15658:2006

Structural Steel Components & Fabrication

Hazira Industrial Zone, Gujarat
