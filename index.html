About the Group

Three decades of building where it's needed most.

Founded on the principle behind our name — ease, and the trust that comes from it — Sahjanand Group grew from a single regional contracting business into a group spanning infrastructure development and industrial manufacturing. Every project we take on is one we intend to still be proud of in twenty years.

We work close to the ground: our engineers, plant managers and site teams are the same people who answer the phone when a client calls.
