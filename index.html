Surat’s Benchmark in Textile Manufacturing & Fabric Innovation

Rooted in Surat — the textile capital of India — Sahjanand commands three decades of manufacturing leadership. Operating high-speed weaving infrastructure, our textile division engineers high-grade synthetic fabrics, precision weaves, and technical textiles with 100% in-house quality control and uncompromising client confidentiality.

Advanced Weaving

High-Speed Synthetic & Filament Weaving

Automated waterjet and high-speed rapier loom operations producing high-density polyester, micro-denier filaments, and composite synthetic fabrics under uniform yarn tension control.

  • Technology: High-Speed Waterjet & Rapier Looms
  • Yarn Compatibility: Polyester, Nylon, Micro & Blended
  • Weave Integrity: Zero Warp-Breakage Tension Calibrated
Commercial Fabrics

Premium Apparel & Commercial Weaves

Precision weaving for domestic garment manufacturers, uniform producers, and commercial apparel houses featuring superior tactile drape, crease recovery, and consistent texture.

  • Patterns: Plain, Twill, Dobby, Oxford & Textured
  • Fabric Feel: Soft Handfeel & High Crease Resistance
  • Supply Mode: Direct Mill-to-Enterprise Bulk Supply
Technical Fabrics

Technical & Industrial Reinforcement

Engineered technical fabrics designed for industrial filtration, tarpaulin substrates, protective covers, and composite base layers engineered to withstand severe tensile strain.

  • Strength: High-Tenacity Synthetic Filament
