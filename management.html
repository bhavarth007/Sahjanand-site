Corporate Governance

A Disciplined Committee-Driven Management Model

At Sahjanand, corporate decisions are never made in isolation from production realities. All major manufacturing investments, raw material procurement policies, and large supply commitments are reviewed by our Technical and Operations Committee.

This ensures that the engineers and plant supervisors closest to daily production have a direct voice in executive strategy before commitments are sealed.
