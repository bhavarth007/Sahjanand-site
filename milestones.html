Our Growth & Journey

Over three and a half decades of disciplined industrial expansion — from foundational weaving and powerlooms in 1989 to integrated Weaving, Yarn, Chemical, and Global Trade operations today.

1989
Inception & Weaving Foundation

Inception in Textiles & Powerloom Weaving

Sahjanand commenced its textile manufacturing operations in Surat, starting with foundational powerloom units. Meeting expanding market demand for high-grade synthetic fabrics, the enterprise rapidly modernized with high-speed Water Jet machines, advanced Rapier looms, and dedicated yarn dyeing lines — setting benchmark standards in fabric strength, weaving consistency, and color fastness.

Textiles Inception Powerloom Units Water Jet Machines Rapier Weaving Yarn Dyeing
2005
Commercial Scale

Strategic Yarn Division Expansion & Market Growth

Achieved transformative commercial growth in the yarn sector. Upgraded yarn processing and handling facilities, expanded centralized warehousing, and established robust distribution networks across Surat and pan-India textile markets — securing long-term institutional supply partnerships with major fabric manufacturers.

Yarn Division Growth Processing Infrastructure Commercial Distribution Pan-India Reach
2021
Technological Modernization

Advanced Yarn Texturising, Twisting & Kasab Jari Machinery

Executed an extensive technological and machinery upgrade across production lines. Commissioned modern yarn texturising units, conventional twisting, high-speed twisting, and yarn covering machinery. Expanded specialized capabilities with the addition of Kasab & Metallic Jari machines — enabling ultra-fine filament processing, premium ethnic zari production, fancy embroidery yarns, and high-tenacity technical weaves.

Yarn Texturising Conventional Twisting High-Speed Twisting Yarn Covering Kasab & Metallic Jari
2025
Strategic Diversification

Chemical Sector Diversification & Sustainable Industrial Growth

Pioneered strategic expansion with the establishment of the Chemical Sector, introducing advanced industrial specialty chemicals, sizing auxiliaries, and eco-friendly textile processing agents. This high-growth vertical reinforces backward integration, powers self-reliance, and accelerates enterprise growth across diversified industrial manufacturing sectors.

Chemical Sector Textile Auxiliaries Specialty Formulations Industrial Growth
Today
Enterprise Milestone

Integrated Multi-Sector Group & MSME ZED Gold Certified

Sahjanand commands a cohesive multi-sector presence spanning the Weaving Sector, Yarn Sector, Chemical Sector, and Global Trade (Import & Export). Officially awarded the Govt. of India MSME ZED Gold Certification (Zero Defect, Zero Effect), Sahjanand represents over three decades of benchmark manufacturing quality, operational safety, and customer trust.
