Our Growth & Journey

Over three and a half decades of disciplined industrial expansion — from foundational weaving and powerlooms in 1989 to integrated Weaving, Yarn, Chemical, and Global Trade operations today.

STEPPED INDUSTRIAL GROWTH GRAPH (1989 — 2026)

Sahjanand Enterprise Evolution Curve

Visualizing three and a half decades of disciplined industrial expansion — showing foundational start in 1989, steady operational consolidation plateaus, upward expansion surges, and multi-sector ZED Gold apex.

1989
Stage 01 of 05 · Level 1 Base

Inception in Textiles & Powerloom Weaving

Inception & Weaving Foundation

Sahjanand commenced its textile manufacturing operations in Surat, starting with foundational powerloom units. Meeting expanding market demand for high-grade synthetic fabrics, the enterprise rapidly modernized with high-speed Water Jet machines, advanced Rapier looms, and dedicated yarn dyeing lines — setting benchmark standards in fabric strength, weaving consistency, and color fastness.

Key Operational Milestones Achieved:
Textiles Inception Powerloom Units Water Jet Machines Rapier Weaving Yarn Dyeing
2005
Stage 02 of 05 · Level 2 Scale

Strategic Yarn Division Expansion & Market Growth

Commercial Scale

Achieved transformative commercial growth in the yarn segment. Upgraded yarn processing and handling facilities, expanded centralized warehousing, and established robust distribution networks across Surat and pan-India textile markets — securing long-term institutional supply partnerships with major fabric manufacturers.

Key Operational Milestones Achieved:
Yarn Division Growth Processing Infrastructure Commercial Distribution Pan-India Reach
2021
Stage 03 of 05 · Level 3 Modernize

Advanced Yarn Texturising, Twisting & Kasab Jari Machinery

Technological Modernization

Executed an extensive technological and machinery upgrade across production lines. Commissioned modern yarn texturising units, conventional twisting, high-speed twisting, and yarn covering machinery. Expanded specialized capabilities with the addition of Kasab & Metallic Jari machines — enabling ultra-fine filament processing, premium ethnic zari production, fancy embroidery yarns, and high-tenacity technical weaves.

Key Operational Milestones Achieved:
Yarn Texturising Conventional Twisting High-Speed Twisting Yarn Covering Kasab & Metallic Jari
2025
Stage 04 of 05 · Level 4 Diversify

Chemical Segment Diversification & Sustainable Industrial Growth

Strategic Diversification

Pioneered strategic expansion with the establishment of the Chemical Segment, introducing advanced industrial specialty chemicals, sizing auxiliaries, and eco-friendly textile processing agents. This high-growth vertical reinforces backward integration, powers self-reliance, and accelerates enterprise growth across diversified industrial manufacturing sectors.

Key Operational Milestones Achieved:
Chemical Segment Textile Auxiliaries Specialty Formulations Industrial Growth
2026 ★
Stage 05 of 05 · Level 5 Apex (MSME ZED Gold)

Integrated Multi-Sector Group & MSME ZED Gold Certified

Enterprise Milestone

Sahjanand commands a cohesive multi-sector presence spanning the Weaving Segment, Yarn Segment, Chemical Segment, and Global Trade (Import & Export). Officially awarded the Govt. of India MSME ZED Gold Certification (Zero Defect, Zero Effect), Sahjanand represents over three decades of benchmark manufacturing quality, operational safety, and customer trust.
