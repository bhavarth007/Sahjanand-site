Milestones

From a single regional contract to a diversified group — the path so far.

1993

Founded in Surat

Sahjanand began as a small contracting outfit taking on local road and drainage work across Surat district.

2001

First manufacturing unit

Opened our first concrete products plant, moving the Group into industrial manufacturing alongside contracting.

2008

Government infrastructure empanelment

Qualified as an approved contractor for state road and water infrastructure projects.

2013

Second and third manufacturing units

Expanded production capacity with two additional facilities, adding steel components and pipe fittings to the product range.

2018

500th project milestone

Crossed 500 completed infrastructure and supply contracts across Gujarat.

2022

Fourth manufacturing unit

Commissioned our newest facility, focused on precast drainage units for municipal projects.

Today

A diversified group

Sahjanand Group now operates across infrastructure development and industrial manufacturing, with over 800 people and four production facilities.
