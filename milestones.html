Our Growth & Journey

Three decades of disciplined manufacturing expansion — from a single local operation to four integrated industrial production units.

1993

Founded in Surat

Sahjanand was established in Surat, Gujarat, founded on the principle of unshakeable business trust and disciplined supply of foundational building materials.

2001

First Dedicated Manufacturing Plant

Commissioned our first automated concrete products plant in Surat district, bringing high-pressure vibro-compaction and precision dimensional control to pavers and blocks.

2008

Institutional Approvals & Empanelment

Achieved technical empanelment and vendor approval for state infrastructure bodies, municipal corporations, and major industrial plant developers across Gujarat.

2013

Major Manufacturing Expansion (Units 2 & 3)

Expanded manufacturing capabilities with the commissioning of our Hazira structural steel fabrication facility and the Sachin GIDC industrial utility fittings plant.

2018

Major Industrial Supply Milestone

Surpassed 500 major commercial and industrial supply consignments delivered with zero recorded dispatch non-conformity across Gujarat.

2022

Fourth Manufacturing Unit Commissioned

Inaugurated our newest specialized precast concrete casting works in Ankleshwar, specifically dedicated to high-load box culverts and municipal drainage infrastructure.

Today

Integrated Industrial Manufacturing Enterprise

Sahjanand operates across four dedicated production facilities with over 800 people, producing more than 40 certified product lines that power Gujarat's industrial landscape.
